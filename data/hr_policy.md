# Human Resources Policy

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees receive vacation on a prorated basis.

Planned vacation of three or more consecutive workdays should be submitted to the employee's manager at least 14 calendar days in advance. Approval depends on business coverage.

Employees also receive 10 company holidays and 5 paid sick days per calendar year.

## Parental Leave
Eligible full-time employees may receive up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave after birth, adoption, or foster placement, subject to HR eligibility verification.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely according to their team's approved operating model. Working from another country requires advance approval from HR and Legal.
