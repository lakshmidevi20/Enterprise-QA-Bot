# Security and Compliance Manual

## Authentication
Multifactor authentication (MFA) is required for all production systems, administrative consoles, and corporate VPN access. Password sharing is prohibited.

## Production Data Access
Access to production customer data follows least-privilege principles. The employee must complete annual security training, have a documented business need, receive manager approval, and receive system-owner approval.

## Phishing
Employees who suspect a phishing email must not click links or open attachments. They must report the message using the corporate phishing-reporting mechanism and notify Security if credentials may have been exposed.

## Customer Data and Vendors
Customer confidential data may be shared with an approved vendor only when the vendor is covered by the required contractual and security review and the data sharing is necessary for the approved business purpose.

## Diagnostic Logs
Customer support diagnostic logs may be retained for up to 30 days unless a legal hold or an approved incident investigation requires longer retention.
