# Engineering Onboarding Guide

## First Week
During the first week, a new engineer should complete identity verification, enroll in MFA, complete security-awareness training, configure the company laptop, read the engineering handbook, and meet the assigned onboarding buddy.

## Development Access
Source-code access is granted after the employee's manager approves membership in the appropriate engineering team.

## Production Access
Production access is not part of default onboarding. Engineers should request it only when their role requires it. A production-access request must follow the Security and Compliance Manual, including documented business need and required approvals.

## First 30 Days
By the end of the first 30 days, engineers should complete the secure-development training and ship at least one reviewed change to a non-production environment.
